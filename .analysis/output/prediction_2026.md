- **Tier A — strong preparation priority:** direct present-module fit plus either five-paper breadth or four-paper breadth with substantial high-mark exposure.

## SECTION D

**Case Study — Compulsory question. (1 × 12 = 12)**

22. **Circular Harvest Foods** **[12 marks]**

Asha and Farah notice that fruit and vegetable vendors discard edible surplus every evening. They propose Circular Harvest Foods, a women-led venture that will buy safe surplus, use solar dryers to make shelf-stable snacks, and employ and train women from nearby self-help groups. A three-week campus pilot attracts repeat buyers, but customers ask for traceable sourcing and recyclable packaging. The founders plan subscription sales, retail partnerships and later overseas sales to Indian-food stores. They must choose a legal form, protect the brand and process know-how, meet food and data-related compliance, refine an MVP, secure finance, obtain incubation support and scale without weakening product safety or social impact.

(a) Identify two entrepreneurial competencies shown by the founders and explain why the opportunity is socially and environmentally responsible. **[3 marks]**

(b) Recommend a suitable ownership form and state two priority legal, food-safety, intellectual-property or data-compliance actions. **[3 marks]**

(c) Outline an appropriate MVP, state one key Business Model Canvas decision, and propose one measurable social-impact indicator. **[3 marks]**

(d) Identify one suitable support organisation, one enabling technology for traceability or operations, and one financing source or mix. Give a one-line reason for each. **[3 marks]**
